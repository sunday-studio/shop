## Steps

1. Open **Analytics**.
2. Pick a range: this month, last month, the last 3 or 6 months, this year, all time, or a custom range.
3. Read the total spent, the number of visits and the average basket.
4. Look at the split **By shop** or **By category**, and tap one to see its receipts.
5. Open **Price changes** to see what got cheaper or more expensive.

## Price changes

Price changes compare your last three buys of a product with the three before, per kilogram, litre or piece. They only show products you have bought several times.

## Prices for one product

Open a product from the Products tab to see its price history, what you usually pay, your recent purchases and the receipt names linked to it. Prices appear once receipt lines are linked to the product.

> **Note:** Spending totals include receipts that still need review. Price figures only use confirmed receipts, so confirm your receipts to include them.
