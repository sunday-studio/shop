Using Shop

Review and fix a receipt

Open a receipt that needs review, fix what's wrong, and confirm.

Steps

  1. 01
    Open the receipt from Home. The image and the lines are on the same screen, and you can open the full-size image to zoom in.
  2. 02
    Compare the printed total with the lines. If they match, there is nothing left to fix.
  3. 03
    Fix a line: tap it, then change its quantity, price or kind (item, discount, deposit, bag or other).
  4. 04
    Link a line to a product, or create a new one. Linking remembers the line's text, so the same text is linked on every next receipt.
  5. 05
    Add a line the scan missed, or delete one it invented.
  6. 06
    Check the date and the shop. Pick a date so the receipt counts in your totals.
  7. 07
    Tap Confirm.

You can confirm at any time, even if the numbers still differ. Sometimes the receipt itself is odd.

After you confirm

  • Products are created or linked for the lines of the receipt. See Products, categories and shops.
  • Items on your shopping list that were on the receipt are checked off. A message tells you how many, with Undo.

If the scan is wrong

Open the receipt's menu for these:

  • Reparse applies the latest rules to the stored text. Re-run OCR reads the image again from scratch. Both ask first, because they drop your edits.
  • Delete receipt removes the receipt and its image.
NoteSpending totals include receipts that still need review, since the money was spent either way. Price comparisons only use confirmed receipts.
