Getting started

Connect the iPhone app

Pair your phone with your server using its address and the password.

Get the app

Shop is not on the App Store yet. For now you build the app yourself and install it on your iPhone.

  1. 01
    Open ios/Shop/Shop.xcodeproj in Xcode 27.
  2. 02
    Select the Shop target, open Signing & Capabilities, and choose your own team. Change the bundle identifier if Xcode asks you to.
  3. 03
    Pick your iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and run the app.

The full setup notes are in IOS-SETUP.md.

Connect

  1. 01
    Open the app and continue past the welcome screen.
  2. 02
    Step 1 asks where your server is. Enter its address, for example http://192.168.1.20:8080 or http://shop.local:8080.
  3. 03
    Tap Test connection. When the app says it found a Shop server, tap Continue.
  4. 04
    Step 2 asks for the pairing password that make setup printed. Enter it and tap Connect.
  5. 05
    When the app says you're connected, scan your first receipt or go to Home.

The first time, the iPhone asks to allow access to your local network. Allow it, or the app cannot reach the server.

The app keeps its own key for this phone in the iPhone Keychain. The password itself is not stored.

Away from home

Tailscale lets your phone reach the server from anywhere without opening any ports on your router.

  1. 01
    Install Tailscale on the server machine and on the iPhone, and sign both into the same tailnet.
  2. 02
    Find the machine's name.
    tailscale status
  3. 03
    Use http://<name>.<tailnet>.ts.net:8080 as the server address in the app.

For HTTPS, run tailscale serve --bg 8080 on the server and use https://<name>.<tailnet>.ts.net instead.

If you lose the password

On the server, make a new one. The app shows this command on the password step too.

docker compose -f deploy/docker-compose.yml exec api shop password generate

Phones that are already connected stay connected. To disconnect all of them as well, add -revoke-devices. One phone can disconnect itself from Settings with Disconnect this iPhone.

NoteFive wrong passwords from one address lock that address out for 15 minutes.
