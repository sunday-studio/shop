# Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA)

> **Draft. This is not legal advice.** It was adapted from an existing template by an AI assistant, not by a lawyer. The project owner must have a lawyer review it before relying on it.

## What this means for you (plain words)

- **You keep your copyright.** You are not handing over ownership of what you write.
- **You give Casprine Assempah (the "Owner") a licence to use it.** The licence is permanent, worldwide, free of charge, cannot be taken back, and is not exclusive (you can still use your own work anywhere else).
- **The Owner can relicense it.** Shop is public under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 licence, but the Owner also runs, or may run, a paid hosted service. Your contribution may be included in that service and may be licensed under other terms, including commercial and closed ones.
- **You also license any patents** that your contribution necessarily uses, for the same purposes.
- **You promise it is yours to give**: you wrote it, or you have the right to submit it. If your employer might own it, you need their permission first.
- **No obligation.** The Owner does not have to accept, use or keep your contribution. You do not have to provide support.
- **Credit stays.** The Owner will not remove your name or authorship from the repository history (commits keep your authorship).

If you do not agree, please do not contribute.
